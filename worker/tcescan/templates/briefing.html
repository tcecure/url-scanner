What this is. TCecure looked only at information your domain and website publish to anyone on the internet — public DNS records, your email policy records, how the site answers a request for its home page, and the certificate it presents. Nothing was logged into, no vulnerability was tested, and no part of your systems was probed. This is not a penetration test or a comprehensive vulnerability assessment.
